What is the difference between Static and Dynamic QR Codes?

Static QR codes encode the destination URL directly into the matrix—they never expire and cannot be changed once printed. Dynamic QR codes route through a short redirection URL, allowing you to change the destination web link anytime without reprinting your flyers, menus, or packaging!

Is AutomatixQR really 100% free to use?

Yes. Generating, styling, customizing logos, and exporting high-resolution PNG & vector SVG QR codes is 100% free with no hidden paywalls, scan caps, or watermarks.

Should I download PNG or Vector SVG for print?

For digital use, websites, and social media, PNG is ideal. For physical printing on billboards, business cards, merchandise, and product labels, use SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)—it can be scaled infinitely to any size without losing crisp sharpness or pixelating.

How do I generate a QR code with my company logo?

Upload your PNG, SVG, or JPEG logo in the 'Center Brand Logo' area of the QR Studio. AutomatixQR centers the logo and automatically increases error correction to Level Q (25%) or Level H (30%) so scanners read it flawlessly.
